Privacy Policy – ProbeClock ProbeClock Widget does not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app connects to the public NASA/JPL Horizons service to retrieve spacecraft distance, one-way light time, mission age, and relative velocity data for Voyager 1, Voyager 2, Pioneer 10, and Pioneer 11. The displayed spacecraft data is referenced to the center of Earth. ProbeClock stores only local app data on the user’s device, including selected unit preferences, selected spacecraft, widget settings, and local emergency backup data. This data is not transmitted to the developer. The app does not require a user account. The app does not collect location data. The app does not use advertising SDKs. The app does not use analytics SDKs. The app does not access contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise device location. The feedback button opens the user’s email app and allows the user to voluntarily send feedback to the developer. The app itself does not automatically collect or transmit email addresses or message content. Local backup data can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s storage in Android settings. Contact: Marcus Stier marcusstier82@gmail.com Last updated: 07 june 2026
